Compare two loan offers without hiding the assumptions.

A lower rate can still take years to recover its upfront fee. SepCalc keeps the source values, timing convention, deterministic calculation, and reusable worksheet together.

Offer A

Principal$300,000
APR6.25%
Term30 years
Upfront fee$6,000
Monthly payment$1,847.15

Offer B

Principal$300,000
APR6.50%
Term30 years
Upfront fee$0
Monthly payment$1,896.20

What the worksheet reveals

Offer A saves $49.05 per month. Recovering its additional $6,000 upfront fee takes about 122.3 months, or 10.2 years.

This break-even comparison excludes taxes, insurance, points with different tax treatment, refinancing, early payoff, and the time value of the upfront fee. Those inputs materially change the decision and belong in the worksheet when applicable.
